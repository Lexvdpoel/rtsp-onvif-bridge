Stream handling
With the relay on, the NVR only ever talks to the virtual camera, and the throughput below can be measured. Turn it off to hand the NVR the upstream URL directly — lighter, but the real camera's address becomes visible and no bitrate can be reported.
Output codec
Re-encoding only happens when the source is not already the codec you picked, so choosing H.264 for an H.264 camera still costs nothing. A real transcode is expensive: software H.265 of a 4 MP stream will occupy several CPU cores per camera. Pick a hardware encoder if your host has one — VA-API and Quick Sync need /dev/dri on the host, NVENC needs the NVIDIA container runtime; the device is passed in automatically. If the stream stays black after switching, drop the audio: not every camera's audio can be carried into RTSP.
Advertised video properties
With this on, the camera probes the source with ffprobe at startup and advertises the real codec, resolution and frame rate; the bitrate falls back to the measured average. The values below are then only used until the probe finishes, or if it fails.
